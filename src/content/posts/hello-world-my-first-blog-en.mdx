# Hello World: Why I Built This Digital Garden and My First Blog Post

Hey there! Really glad you stumbled across my little corner of the internet. 👋

As programmer tradition dictates, every meaningful new beginning starts with a simple `Hello, World!`. Since this is my very first real post on this personal site, let's skip dry academic lectures. Grab a coffee, and let's have a casual chat about why I built this space and what I've been hacking on lately.

---

## Why build an independent website in 2026?

People often ask: *"With so many social networks, dev communities, and freelancing platforms around, why spend weekends writing code to host your own site?"*

My answer is straightforward: **On third-party platforms, you are a tenant; on your own domain, you are the homeowner.**

Most platforms lock you into algorithmic feeds, character limits, or rigid commercial templates. On service marketplaces, every page is plastered with pricing tiers, delivery days, and formal scopes—feeling more like a cold legal contract than a creative space. But as an engineer, beyond client deliverables, I craved a quiet corner with zero algorithmic noise. A place where I can showcase projects I'm genuinely proud of and write down hard-earned lessons.

This website is my personal digital garden.

---

## What do I actually work on?

For those getting to know me, my core foundation has always been **Java backend engineering**.
My main focus is Java backend development, alongside frontend and full-stack work. This space is where I want to share development notes, practical experiments, and topics I am still learning.

Yet curiosity keeps pushing me forward:
- **Venturing into Modern Web & Full-Stack**: People used to joke that backend devs build interfaces that "work, but look like 1998." I decided to change that, diving deep into modern web standards, React, Tailwind CSS, and Astro. Breathing life into an interface from design to subtle interaction feels uniquely satisfying.
- **Crafting Everyday Productivity Tools**: During testing and frontend debugging, fragile DOM selectors and manual style extraction for LLMs were constant pain points. Instead of tolerating tedious workarounds, I built **PixPage - Smart Element Picker Assistant** (now published on the Chrome Web Store). Building a real tool that you and other developers rely on daily is pure developer joy.
- **Pragmatic AI Tooling**: I am exploring practical AI API integrations and automation. I don't buy into wild hype, but treat AI as a tireless copilot that frees up brainpower for real problem-solving.

---

## What will you find on this blog?

I want this space to remain authentic and practical:
1. **Real-world Debugging Stories**: Strange concurrency deadlocks, mysterious memory leaks, and query optimization battles worth remembering.
2. **Toolbox & Recommendations**: Tested open-source utilities, developer setups, and insights from building Chrome extensions.
3. **Engineering Philosophy & Reflections**: Candid thoughts on software craftsmanship, simplicity, and architectural trade-offs.
